Cookie Policy

No tracking, no banner

Last Updated: 26/06/2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Shaka Fuerte SL uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. In short: this website does not set its own cookies, and no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies are used.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to improve performance, or to collect information about browsing activity.

2. Cookies Used by This Website

This website does not set its own (first-party) cookies. We do not use:

  • Analytics or statistics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics);
  • Advertising, marketing or profiling cookies;
  • Social media tracking cookies;
  • Any cookies used to identify or track visitors.

You can browse this website without any cookies being installed by us.

3. Third-Party Maps (Google Maps)

Our location pages can display an interactive Google Maps map. To protect your privacy, the map is not loaded automatically. You first see a static preview image, and the interactive map loads only if you click "Show interactive map".

This means no Google cookies are set unless you actively choose to load the map. By clicking to load it, you consent to Google Maps being loaded, which may set its own third-party cookies on your device. These cookies are controlled by Google and governed by Google's own privacy and cookie policies: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Fonts

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. This involves a request to Google's servers but does not install cookies on your device.

5. Why There Is No Cookie Banner

Because this website sets no non-essential cookies on its own, and because the only third-party content that may set cookies (Google Maps) is loaded solely after you explicitly choose to enable it, no general cookie consent banner is required. Consent for the map is collected at the exact moment you decide to load it.

6. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Most browsers allow you to block third-party cookies or to be notified before a cookie is stored. Please note that blocking cookies may affect the functionality of third-party content such as embedded maps.

7. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page together with the revised effective date.

8. Contact

For any questions about this Cookie Policy, you can contact us at info@shaka-fuerte.com. This policy is governed by the laws of Spain and should be read together with our Privacy Policy.
