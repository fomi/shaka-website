Who We Are

Born From a Simple Idea

Shaka Fuerte started five years ago from an idea by Matteo: build a shop and a school that give riders a more personal, flexible service, free from the rigid opening and closing hours of a normal rental.

The concept is simple. No queues, no fixed slots, no waiting for the rental to open. Just grab your gear, load your car, and ride where you want, when you want.

That's what Shaka Fuerte is really about: the freedom to ride on your own terms, whether you're here to learn, to rent, or just to feel the wind on the water.
