The Shaka Shop

Everything You Need to Ride

Whether you're chasing wind or waves, our shop has you covered. We rent top-brand equipment for every wind and water sport, with gear you can swap anytime to match the conditions.

Based at C.C. Botánico in Costa Calma, with easy car access for loading and unloading. Every rental includes a free universal roofrack, and our team is always on hand to help you pick the right setup.

Not just rental: step inside for streetwear, beachwear and accessories, plus ILOVEBIKES bike rental and the Beach House pizzeria to refuel after your session.

Renting Is Easy

How It Works

1. Come in the shop
2. Choose your gear and how long
3. Load up your car
(free roofrack included)
4. Ride everywhere
5. Bring back your gear at the end of your holiday
