The Shaka Method

Tailored to You

Whether it's your very first time on a board or you're chasing your next move, our lessons are tailored to you. Our instructors follow every student from a support boat, so you're never alone on the water, at any level, from absolute beginner to advanced.

Giorgio, Mitch and Henri are VDWS-certified instructors with over 35 years of combined teaching experience. They know how to read the conditions, read the rider, and get you progressing fast while having fun.

The bay of Costa Calma is the perfect place to learn: flat, shallow water protected from the wind. Safe, calm and forgiving. Perfect for kids too.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

No experience needed. Our lessons are designed for complete beginners, taking you step by step from your very first day.

Yes — all our lessons are all-inclusive: board, sail or wing, lifejacket and wetsuit are all provided.

We keep groups small so you get real attention. Windsurf lessons are in small groups, and wingfoil lessons are limited to a maximum of two people per instructor.

Our instructors speak English, Italian, German, Spanish, Czech, Slovak and Polish.

For group lessons, windsurf starts from 8 years old and wingfoil from 10. For younger children, we can arrange private lessons.

If there's no wind or the ocean conditions aren't suitable for the lesson, we reschedule it for a different time or day. If it becomes completely impossible, you'll receive a full 100% refund.
